An Interior Design Company specializes in creating functional and aesthetically pleasing spaces by blending design principles with the needs and preferences of their clients. These companies offer a range of services, from conceptualizing design ideas to executing and styling interiors for homes, offices, retail spaces, and more

Company Overview

At JRKINTERIOR, we are dedicated to transforming spaces into beautiful, functional environments that reflect the individuality of our clients. Our team of experienced interior designers works closely with you to understand your vision and style, delivering personalized designs that maximize both beauty and practicality.

Our Founder

Founded by Sanjay Singh, a passionate and visionary interior designer, JRKINTERIOR was born out of a desire to create spaces that harmonize both style and function. With a background in Interior Designer, 8 Years Experience ,Sanjay Singh has dedicated their career to mastering the art of interior design, blending traditional aesthetics with modern trends. After years of working in the design industry, Sanjay Singh recognized the growing demand for personalized design solutions that truly cater to the unique needs of clients. This inspired the creation of JRKINTERIOR, with the mission to offer tailored, innovative design solutions for every project.
